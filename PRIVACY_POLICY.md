# Privacy Policy for معركة أحفاد الصحابة: مسابقات (Ahfad Al-Sahaba: Battle)

**Last Updated:** November 3, 2026

Welcome to **معركة أحفاد الصحابة: مسابقات** ("Ahfad Al-Sahaba: Battle", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our game collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you play **معركة أحفاد الصحابة: مسابقات**, we collect minimal information necessary to deliver gameplay features and competitive leaderboards:

- **Profile Information:** Nickname / Display Name chosen by the user, avatar choice, and player level.
- **Account Information (Optional):** If you register or sign in, we collect your email address for account recovery, authentication, and communication.
- **Gameplay Data:** Quiz scores, match records, rank points, achievements, and leaderboard standings.
- **Technical & Diagnostics Data:** Basic device information (OS version, device model) and anonymous crash logs to ensure app stability and performance.

We **DO NOT** collect sensitive personal data such as physical location (GPS), biometric data, financial details, or contacts.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
- Enabling multiplayer matches, matchmaking, and real-time quiz battles.
- Maintaining player profiles, high scores, and global rankings.
- Sending transactional service messages (e.g. welcome notifications, account confirmations).
- Improving game stability, fixing bugs, and enhancing user experience.

We **NEVER** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertising brokers.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Encryption in Transit:** All communications between the mobile application and our backend servers (Supabase) are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
- **Secure Infrastructure:** Your gameplay statistics and authentication records are securely stored with role-based access policies (Row Level Security - RLS).
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The application does not contain third-party ad networks or tracking trackers.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy

Our application is designed as an educational, cultural, and historical quiz game for general audiences (aged 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data:
- You may update your profile name and avatar at any time in the app settings.
- You can request complete deletion of your account and associated gameplay records by emailing us at: **omnitech9n@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete My Account". All data will be permanently wiped within 7 business days.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
